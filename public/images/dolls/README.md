# Doll Collection Images — Music Room

Drop doll images here to replace the emoji plushies on the Music Room shelf.
If any file is missing or fails to load, that doll stays as its emoji — you can replace them one at a time.

The shelf now has **two rows**: row 1 holds dolls 1–5, row 2 holds dolls 6–9.

## Recommended Format & Size

**Format:** PNG with a transparent background (required for best look on the shelf).
The dolls sit on a brown wooden shelf — a transparent background blends them in naturally. A white background will look like floating white boxes.

**Size:** 256 × 256 px.
The dolls are displayed at roughly 96 px (`text-8xl`) and scale up to ~120 px on hover. A 256 px source keeps them sharp at both sizes.

**Style tip:** Soft, cute, plushie-style art works best. Flat-shaded chibi or pastel kawaii styles look great against the warm shelf lighting. High-detail realistic images may look out of place.

## Free Sources

- **Flaticon.com** → search "stuffed animal", "plushie", "kawaii"
- **Freepik.com** → transparent PNG stickers
- **Vecteezy.com** → cute plushie vectors (export as PNG)
- **Etsy / Creative Market** — premium sticker packs often include plushie art
